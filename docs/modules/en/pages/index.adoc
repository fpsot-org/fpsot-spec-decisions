= Put a decision type under the decisions specification
:page-type: how-to
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This guide takes a public body from one automated decision type to a conforming one.
Each step cites the rule it meets.
It is not legal advice, and the page of questions for counsel lists the points that a lawyer must settle.
The files in `examples/` follow a fictional housing benefit through every step.

== Describe the decision type

List the laws that govern the decision, and pick the profiles, under DEC-011.
Always declare `core`.
A Canadian federal institution adds `ca-federal`, a Québec body adds `ca-qc`, and a French administration adds `fr`.
Add `eu-ai-high-risk` when your counsel says that the European regulation applies.

Set the impact level from your impact assessment, under DEC-013.
Under `ca-federal`, it is the level of your published Algorithmic Impact Assessment.
Name each assessment that your profiles ask for, under DEC-014.

Write the description of the decision type, under DEC-010.
The file `examples/decision-type.yaml` shows one.

== Write the reason scheme

Publish a concept scheme of reason codes under the vocabularies specification, under DEC-050.
Give each code a label and a definition in English and in French.
Name a factor in each code, such as `household-income-above-limit`, never a verdict, under DEC-051.
The file `examples/reason-codes.jsonld` shows a small scheme.

== Write the rules

Implement the decision contract, under DEC-020 to DEC-023.
Return `insufficient-information` when a fact is missing, never a refusal, under DEC-025.
Return `referred` when the law leaves room for discretion or hardship, under DEC-026.

Give each rule version its metadata, under DEC-030 to DEC-033.
Put amounts and thresholds in a dated parameter file, under DEC-037.
Keep every version that an open decision may need in the artifact, under DEC-034.

Write the scenarios with your policy experts, in English and in French, under DEC-080.
Freeze a corpus of cases for each version before it takes effect, under DEC-081.

== Run the decision service

Store one record for each result, under DEC-040, and seal it under the signed records specification, under DEC-045.
Keep the input values in the record, and only identifiers and codes in the events, under DEC-042 and DEC-044.

At levels 3 and 4 of `ca-federal`, the service refers each result to an officer first, under DEC-061.
The officer confirms or changes it in a review step, with a justification, under DEC-062.
The final record revises the referred one, under DEC-043.

== Give the notice

Render the notice from the record, in the language of the person, under DEC-056.
State that automation took part, and give the recourse channel, as your profiles ask, under DEC-057.
Seal the rendered notice, and keep a notice record that names its seal.

== Check the result

Run the example check on a copy of the repository that holds your files under the same names.

[source,text]
----
pip install --require-hashes -r examples/requirements.txt
python3 examples/check-examples.py path/to/your/copy
----

A pass shows that your records and review steps hold together under the structural rules.
The same holds for your notices, your events and your features.
It does not test your rules, which the decision conformance suite of DEC-083 will do.

= Decision records
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

No law sets a complete schema for the record of one decision.
Taken together, though, they ask for the same facts.
The federal directive of Canada asks institutions to document their automated decisions.
Québec guidance says that a body must give the personal information that it used, on request.
Under the European regulation on artificial intelligence, a high-risk system must log its events automatically.

This page sets one record that can serve all of them.
Most of its fields belong to other {abbr-fpsot-first} specifications, and this page cites them.
Only the fields that are specific to decisions are new.

== The record

[[DEC-040]]
*DEC-040.* A decision service MUST create one decision record for each result of a rule provider, whatever its kind.
The record MUST follow the class `DecisionRecord` of `model/decisions.yaml`.
It MUST hold the fields of this table, and take each field from the specification that owns it.

[cols="3,3,2",options="header"]
|===
|Field |Owner |Rule
|`id` |Observability: the identifier of the business event |OBS-024, DEC-041
|`objects` |Observability: object references |OBS-073
|`decisionType`, `decisionTypeVersion` |Vocabularies for the code, this specification for the release |VOC-064, DEC-010
|`profiles`, `impactLevel` |This specification |DEC-011 to DEC-013
|`legalAuthorities` |This specification |DEC-031
|`ruleSet`, `parameterSets` |This specification: the plan of the activity |DEC-030, DEC-037
|`effectiveDate`, `recordTime` |This specification |DEC-022
|`startedAt`, `completedAt`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt` |Observability and data types: instants in {abbr-utc-first} |OBS-030, DAT-010
|`inputs`, `presumptions` |This specification |DEC-042, DEC-033
|`resultKind`, `outcome`, `recommendedOutcome`, `referralReason`, `missingFacts` |This specification, with codes under the vocabularies specification |DEC-024 to DEC-026
|`amount` |Data types: money |DAT-050
|`reasonScheme`, `reasons` |Vocabularies for the codes, this specification for the parameters |DEC-050 to DEC-055
|`automationDegree`, `reviewStepIds` |This specification, with reviewers as identity actors |DEC-060, IAM-091
|`predecessorId`, `changeCause` |This specification: a revision |DEC-043
|`trace` |This specification, by reference |DEC-048
|Integrity |Log: seal, manifest and transparency log |DEC-045
|===

The model follows link:https://www.w3.org/TR/2013/REC-prov-o-20130430/[PROV-O].
A record is an entity that one decision activity generated.
That activity used the inputs and followed the rule version as its plan.
The service and each reviewer were its agents.
A later record is a revision of an earlier one.

The model follows DMO-010 to DMO-013 and DMO-020 to DMO-024 of the data models specification.
Its valid examples sit in `examples/`, and its test builds the invalid ones from broken copies.
The example folder of DMO-017 and the generated forms of DMO-014 and DMO-015 will come with the conformance suite.

The effective date, the record time and the time of the run are three different facts.
A replay needs all three.

[[DEC-041]]
*DEC-041.* The `id` of a decision record MUST be the `org.fpsot.event.id` of the business event of that decision, under OBS-024.
The CloudEvents event of DEC-072 MUST carry the same value as its `id`.
The seal manifest of the record MUST carry it as its `id`, under LOG-019.

One identifier then joins the record, its event, its seal and its entry in the transparency log.

== Inputs

[[DEC-042]]
*DEC-042.* The record MUST keep the value of each input fact, or an immutable reference to a stored copy.
A stored copy MUST live at least as long as the record.
The record MUST NOT keep a hash of a fact in place of its value or its reference.
Each input MUST give its source, the date when the source stated it and how the body collected it.

Government guidance on article 65.2 says that a Québec body must give the personal information that it used, on request.
A hash cannot give that information back.
A reference to a source system that can change or destroy the value cannot either.

== Revisions

[[DEC-043]]
*DEC-043.* A decision service MUST NOT change or delete a decision record before its retention ends.
To correct, review, reconsider or reprocess a decision, it MUST create a new record.
The new record MUST name the old one in `predecessorId`, give a `changeCause` code and hold every field again.
A record that revises a `referred` record with a `decided` result MUST name at least one review step.
A record that revises an `insufficient-information` record MUST rest on new inputs or on a review step.
It MUST NOT rest on elapsed time alone.

Each new record then stands alone.
A referral or a request for information never turns into an automated result by itself.
Change causes come from a scheme under the vocabularies specification, such as `human-review`, `correction`, `appeal` or `retroactive-law`.
The file `examples/decision-record-2.json` revises `examples/decision-record-1.json` after a human review.
OBS-078 sets the same principle for business events.

== Three stores

[[DEC-044]]
*DEC-044.* A deploying body MUST keep three stores of decision data apart.

* The decision record holds the full inputs.
Only the roles that the deploying body names may read it, under IAM-050.
* The business event of DEC-071 holds identifiers, codes and the rule version.
It MUST NOT hold the value of an input fact, an amount or a name.
* Statistics for monitoring come from the events and hold no direct identifier of a person.
They MUST replace each such identifier with a keyed pseudonym for one purpose, under OBS-093.

A system MUST NOT copy an input value into an event or into a statistics store.

Each store has its own purpose, its own readers and its own retention.
A copy of an income in an event would spread it to every reader of the log.

== Integrity

[[DEC-045]]
*DEC-045.* A deploying body MUST seal each decision record under the signed records specification.
Its series of decision records MUST claim at least the `seal-log` profile of LOG-002.
The salted hash of LOG-031 is then the only data about the record in the transparency log.
When the body destroys a record, it MUST destroy the salt with it, under LOG-081.

A sealed record proves that nobody changed it after the decision.
The log proves that the body showed the same record to every reader.
The salt keeps any personal information out of the log.

== Retention

[[DEC-046]]
*DEC-046.* Each decision record, review step and notice record MUST have a retention rule.
That rule MUST name its rule of the retention schedule.
OBS-102 sets that duty for log stores, and this rule applies it to decision data.
A legal hold under OBS-103 MUST stop destruction, and each destruction MUST leave an event under OBS-104.
The body MUST keep each rule version, parameter file and reason scheme release while a record that used it remains.

Only the schedule of the deploying body sets how long a record stays.
The retention profile of OBS-101 does not apply to decision records.
The comparison data gives the retention periods that the research found, each _to be verified_.
The page xref:questions-for-counsel.adoc[Questions for counsel] asks a lawyer which floors apply.

== Replay

[[DEC-047]]
*DEC-047.* A decision service MUST be able to replay a decision in two modes.

* As decided: the original rule version, the original parameters, the original inputs and the original context.
The result and its reasons MUST be the same as in the original record.
For a record that follows review steps, the replay MUST give the result and reasons that the reviewers saw.
It MUST then check that the recorded review steps lead from that result to the record.
A replay does not reproduce the judgment of a reviewer.
* As corrected: rectified inputs, or the rule version that the current record time selects, or both.
The result MUST be a new record that revises the original under DEC-043.

A replay as decided shows what the system did.
A replay as corrected shows what it should have done once the facts or the law changed.
A person in Québec may ask to correct the information that a decision used.
The second mode is then a common case.

== Traces

[[DEC-048]]
*DEC-048.* A rule provider MAY produce a trace of each run, such as the rule elements that applied.
When it does, the record MUST name the trace by {abbr-uri-first} and digest, and MUST NOT hold it inline.
The deploying body MUST keep the trace as long as the record.

link:https://openfisca.readthedocs.io/en/latest/openfisca-python-api/tracer.html[OpenFisca] records the value of each parameter for each period in its trace.
A trace by reference keeps the record small and readable, while the detail stays available for a review.

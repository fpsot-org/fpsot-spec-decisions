# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT decisions specification.

The specification covers software that makes an individual administrative decision, or helps a person make one, whatever technique computes the result: the description of decision types with their jurisdiction profiles, impact levels and impact assessments; a decision contract with typed inputs, an evaluation context and four result kinds, described in a language-neutral model and a plain Java binding; the metadata, effective dating and resolution of rule versions, with dated parameters and retroactive changes; the decision record, its revisions, its integrity, its retention and its replay; reason codes, counterfactual values, notices and general explanations; human review steps, gating by impact level, dual review, engagement evidence and override statistics; the responses and events of decision services; and scenarios, frozen corpora, invariants and a conformance suite for rule providers, including providers that run decision models. It includes a LinkML model, jurisdiction profiles, event schemas, example files and the scripts that check them.

It does not cover how a system discovers, defaults or overrides a rule provider, which a future FPSOT specification on capabilities will cover; the content of the rules of any program; the choice of a rules engine; business events, identities, concept schemes, problem details, seals and the transparency log, which other FPSOT specifications set; or legal advice.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
